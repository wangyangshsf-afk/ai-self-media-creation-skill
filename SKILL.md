# China Social Account Niche Strategy

Help a creator choose a sustainable account niche, not a list of post ideas. The only strategic deliverable is exactly three ranked niche recommendations: one primary niche and two alternatives.

## Scope

- In scope: assess account positioning and recommend niches for Douyin, WeChat Channels, and Xiaohongshu, individually or across the platforms the user names.
- Out of scope: topic/title ideation, scripts, copywriting, calendars, production plans, cover design, account operation, and publishing. Do not add these as unsolicited sections.
- A niche is a durable audience promise defined by **who it serves + what recurring need it addresses + the creator's distinctive way of serving it**. It is more specific than a broad industry and broader than a single post topic.
- Do not claim a niche is guaranteed to go viral, grow, or monetize.

## Workflow

1. Extract the positioning inputs already supplied: target audience, creator expertise/experience, distinctive resources or proof, goals, platform(s), content format and production constraints, monetization constraints, and any account performance evidence.
2. If a missing input would materially change the recommendation, ask one compact batch of no more than four focused questions. Do not ask again for information already given. If the user wants an immediate answer or the gaps are manageable, state assumptions and proceed.
3. Form a small candidate set of durable niches, then compare them using the decision rubric below. Do not equate current popularity with strategic fit.
4. Return exactly one primary niche and two alternatives, ranked. Explain why the primary wins and what trade-off makes each alternative secondary. If evidence is weak, lower confidence and state what would change the decision.
5. End with one concise validation action that tests the niche choice (for example, a small audience interview or a limited content experiment). Do not turn this into topic recommendations or a content calendar.

## Decision rubric

Score each candidate from 1 to 5 on the following dimensions, using the user's evidence and clearly labeled inference. Use the weighted total as a comparison aid, not as false precision:

| Dimension | Weight | What to assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Positioning fit | 25% | Match between audience, creator's expertise/proof, and the promise the account can credibly make |
| Audience need | 20% | How concrete, recurring, and consequential the audience problem or aspiration is |
| Differentiation | 20% | Whether the creator has a defensible perspective, method, access, identity, or proof rather than generic coverage |
| Sustainable supply | 20% | Ability to produce useful material consistently within available time, skills, access, and format constraints |
| Goal and business fit | 15% | Fit with the stated growth, reputation, lead-generation, sales, or other goal and with acceptable monetization paths |

Platform fit is a required cross-check, not a substitute for the rubric. Describe only relevant differences:

- Douyin: assess whether the niche can communicate its value quickly in video, demonstrate a clear payoff, and sustain attention; avoid assuming entertainment is mandatory.
- WeChat Channels: assess trust, expertise, relationship/community, and compatibility with the user's WeChat ecosystem or intended audience; do not assume the creator has private-traffic reach unless stated.
- Xiaohongshu: assess search/discovery intent, practical or aspirational utility, visual/storytelling fit, and whether the creator can provide credible experience; do not reduce the platform to beauty or shopping.

When recommending a cross-platform niche, keep the core audience promise consistent and explain any meaningful platform-specific fit difference. Do not invent platform rankings, audience sizes, conversion rates, or trend statistics.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- Separate **用户已提供**, **可核验依据**, and **策略判断**. Never present an inference as a measured fact.
- Use live research or TrendRadar/MCP data only if available and relevant. Tool availability is optional; this skill must also work from user-provided positioning alone.
- Trend or ranking data may inform demand/distribution context, but is not proof of creator-account fit, durable demand, or monetization. Note source and observation date when using time-sensitive evidence.
- If no current evidence is available, say so plainly and make a reasoned strategic recommendation without calling it a hot trend.
- State confidence as high, medium, or low with a short reason. Identify the key assumption whose failure would most alter the recommendation.

## Output format

Use concise Chinese by default. Adapt wording to the user's language if they request another. Return this structure and no extra ideation sections:

### 定位理解
Summarize the target audience, creator advantage, goal, platform(s), and material constraints in a few lines. Mark assumptions explicitly.

### 推荐结果
**主赛道：{specific niche}**
- 账号承诺：服务谁、解决何种 recurring need、凭什么由该账号来做。
- 推荐理由：connect the strongest positioning fit, audience need, differentiation, sustainability, and goal fit.
- 平台适配：address only the platform(s) named by the user.
- 主要风险/边界：identify the most important drawback or credibility boundary.
- 评分：show five rubric scores and weighted total; explain briefly that scores are comparative judgments, not market measurements.

**备选赛道 A：{specific niche}**
- 适合条件与优势：state when this would beat the primary choice.
- 相对取舍：explain why it ranks second.

**备选赛道 B：{specific niche}**
- 适合条件与优势：state when this would beat the primary choice.
- 相对取舍：explain why it ranks third.

### 结论
State the primary choice in one sentence, confidence (high/medium/low) and why, the key assumption to validate, and one low-cost validation action. Do not propose any content topics, titles, scripts, or post ideas.

## Quality check

Before responding, verify:

- There is exactly one primary niche and exactly two alternatives.
- Each niche is concrete, audience-led, distinct from the other two, and durable enough to guide an account over time.
- The primary is selected for fit and feasibility, not merely trendiness or market size.
- Platform claims match the named platform(s) and are not fabricated.
- Facts, user inputs, and strategic judgments are distinguishable.
- No topic ideas, post titles, scripts, publishing guidance, or unsolicited content package slipped into the response.
